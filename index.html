How to Size Your Nails

It takes about 5 minutes. Just measure each nail in millimeters.

✦ Clear tape✦ A pen✦ A ruler with mm

Tips for the perfect fit

  • Measure just the nail, not the skin around it.
  • Between two sizes? Round up (15.5 mm → 16 mm).
  • Measure every finger: your left and right hands are often a little different.
  • No ruler? Send us a photo of your fingers and thumb next to a dime, taken from right above, and we’ll work it out.
